FAQ

Are these tests really free?

Yes. The result is on screen. You only enter an email if you want the result sent to you.

Are they official or certified?

No. They are indicators written by Articulate English to help you choose a course. They are not Cambridge, IELTS, TOEFL or Duolingo results and cannot be used as proof of level.

Do you store my answers?

Only if you ask for the result by email — then we store your address, your level and your score so we can help you later. See the privacy policy. The kids' test stores nothing.

Can I book a lesson from here?

Yes. After your result you can request one of our two free intro sessions a week, or book a paid package directly.
