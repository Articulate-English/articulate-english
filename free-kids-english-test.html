    FAQ

    Is this test free?

    Yes. No account, no payment. Your child's result is on screen; if you want it sent to you, you can give the parent's email address at the end.

    Do you keep my child's answers?

    Your child's answers stay in your browser and are never stored or sent to us. We never ask for your child's name or contact details.

    Can I get the result by email?

    Yes. At the end you can give your own (the parent's) email address and we will send the result to you. That is the only address we ask for, and both email boxes start unticked.

    Is it an official exam?

    No. It is a friendly indicator to show where your child is comfortable. It is not a Cambridge Young Learners or equivalent result.

    My child is younger than 7 or older than 12. Can they try?

    Yes — the questions are just a guide. If they find it too hard or too easy, that itself is useful information.

    Can my child take lessons with you?

    Yes. Under-18 students are booked by a parent or guardian. Email hello@articulate.ee to ask.
