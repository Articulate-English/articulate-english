    How the test works

    The questions are grouped into five bands, A1 to C1. Starting at A1, you must get at least 60% of a band right to move on to the next one; the highest band you pass is your estimated level. That mirrors how a real placement interview works — we stop at the point where the questions stop being comfortable.

    Free English level test — FAQ

    Is this English level test free?

    Yes. It takes about ten minutes and the result is shown on screen. You do not need an account.

    What levels does it cover?

    The CEFR scale from A1 to C1, with questions at each band plus a short reading passage.

    Is this a certified or official test?

    No. It is an indicator to help you choose the right course. It is not a Cambridge, IELTS, TOEFL or Duolingo result and cannot be used as proof of level for a university or employer.

    Do I have to give my email address?

    No. Your result is on screen either way. Both email boxes start unticked — the first only sends your result, the second only agrees to marketing.

    How accurate is it?

    It is a good indicator of grammar, vocabulary and reading, but it does not test speaking or writing. A short call gives a far better picture of spoken level.

    Can my child take a test?

    The kids' test is being prepared. For under-18 students we always book through a parent or guardian — see our safeguarding policy.
