Safeguarding & minors

How we keep young learners safe, and what we ask of parents and students.

Under-18 students must be booked by a parent or guardian. A child cannot conclude a lesson contract with us directly. The booking adult is responsible for payment, for being reachable, and for supporting the student's attendance.

Who we teach

We teach adults and young learners. Where a student is under 18, the following applies on top of our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

Parental responsibility

  • A parent or legal guardian makes the booking and provides their own contact details.
  • The parent or guardian confirms the student's age and that the student may take part.
  • We communicate with the parent or guardian, not privately with the student.
  • A parent or guardian can ask what we hold on the student at any time, and can ask for it to be corrected or deleted.

1-to-1 teaching safeguards

  • Lessons take place in a live video session on the platform we name in the booking — never in a private chat room, and never by personal messaging app.
  • We do not ask students for personal or home details beyond what the booking requires.
  • We do not give out personal contact details to students, and we do not connect with students on personal social media.
  • Lesson recordings, where offered, are provided only to the booking parent or guardian through the account area. Students do not receive raw recordings by default.
  • Recording a lesson by either party without the other's written agreement is a breach of our terms.

Data protection for children

Children's personal data is treated with extra care:

  • We collect the minimum needed to teach and to schedule — no profiling, no advertising, no sale of data.
  • Where local law requires a minimum age for a child to consent to data processing (for example GDPR Article 8, which is between 12 and 16 depending on the country), we rely on the parent or guardian's consent instead.
  • Where a child is in South Africa, we follow POPIA; where in the United Kingdom, the Age Appropriate Design Code; where in the United States, COPPA for under-13s.
  • You can read the full detail in our Privacy Policy.

Reporting a concern

If you have any concern about a student's welfare, a lesson, or the conduct of anyone in our teaching, contact us immediately:

hello@articulate.ee — mark the subject Safeguarding. We treat these reports as urgent and respond within one working day.

If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services first.

Working with schools and organisations

Where a school, employer or other organisation books lessons for its students or staff, the organisation is the contracting party and is responsible for any checks or consents its own policies require. We are happy to provide our credentials and references on request.

Our commitment

We are a single-teacher practice, so the person teaching your child is named on our Impressum and is the same person who handles every safeguarding report. There is no hand-off to a third-party tutor pool.

This page is information about our policies, not legal advice. If you need formal assurance for a school or insurer, ask and we will provide our written safeguarding statement.
