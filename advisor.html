Which package fits you?

Five questions, no sign-up, no payment. The advisor matches your goal, level, timetable and budget to a package, and tells you why.

1. What is the main goal?

This decides what we work on in the sessions.

2. What is your level now?

Honest is better than generous — we plan from where you are.

3. Is there a deadline?

A near deadline changes the intensity we recommend.

4. How many 30-minute lessons a week?

This is the biggest factor in how fast you progress.

5. How much do you want to invest each month?

We will not recommend something that strains you.

This advisor is a guide built from your answers. It is not an English assessment and it does not take payment. A short free call is how we confirm your level and fit. EU/UK consumers have a statutory 14-day right to withdraw from a distance contract — see Consumer rights & withdrawal.
