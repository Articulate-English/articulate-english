About Articulate English

Your teacher, your curriculum, your results.

Who teaches you

Articulate English is a one-teacher online English school run by William Gray Thomason. Every lesson is taught by the same person who answers your email — there is no call centre and no hand-off to a pool of strangers.

William is a CELTA-certified English teacher with 10 years of teaching experience, and a multilingual speaker who has taught adults, professionals, exam candidates and young learners online. Lessons are live, one-to-one video sessions, so the work is always built around you rather than a script.

What you get

  • A named teacher who plans your route and remembers your goals.
  • A structured CEFR path from A1 to C1, with placement and progress checks.
  • Exam-simulation speaking practice for the FCE (B2 First) and IELTS tests.
  • Live AI translation into your home language when a concept needs it.
  • Written feedback after sessions, and recording with replay on eligible plans.
  • 30-minute sessions of focused 1:1 teaching — no group classes, no filler.

Exam simulation, honestly described

Our speaking decks are original practice material modelled on the exam format — the parts, timing and task types — so a real test feels familiar rather than surprising. We are not affiliated with or endorsed by Cambridge University Press & Assessment, the British Council or IDP, and we do not use past-paper questions. "FCE" and "B2 First" are used only to name the exam being practised.

How lessons work

Booking, rescheduling, cancellation and refunds are set out in Delivery & refunds. Lessons are delivered by live video call, with materials and feedback in your account area.
