Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-23

Plain English summary. We collect the minimum information needed to teach you and schedule your lessons. We never sell your data. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

1. Who we are

Articulate English is a one-teacher language school. The data controller is the operator named on our Impressum. You can reach us at hello@articulate.ee.

2. What we collect

  • Contact details — your name and email address when you book a lesson or write to us.
  • Booking details — the time, duration and status of lessons you schedule.
  • Lesson records — notes and progress assessments needed to teach you.
  • Payment records — transaction references, where a lesson is paid for.
  • Technical logs — standard server logs (IP address, browser type) for security and troubleshooting.

3. Why we process it

We process your data to: deliver lessons you have booked; manage scheduling; keep payment and attendance records required for tax purposes; respond to your enquiries; and secure the site against abuse.

Where the law requires a legal basis, we rely on the performance of our contract with you (the lesson you booked) and on our legitimate interest in running a secure school. We do not carry out automated decision-making or profiling.

4. What we never do

  • We do not sell or rent your personal data to anyone.
  • We do not use your data for advertising or share it with advertisers.
  • We do not send marketing messages without your explicit consent.

5. Third parties we use

We use a small number of processors, each only for the purpose stated:

  • Scheduling — our booking provider, to create and manage your appointments.
  • Payment — our payment provider, to take payment for lessons. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Video lessons — the video-conferencing provider used for 1-on-1 lessons.
  • Hosting — our static site host, which serves these pages.

Some of these providers may process data outside your country. Where that happens, we rely on the safeguards those providers offer under their own privacy terms.

6. Cookies and local storage

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. The site stores a single local preference (your chosen colour theme) in your browser's local storage. This never leaves your device and you can clear it at any time.

7. How long we keep it

  • Booking and lesson records — as long as you are a student, plus the period required for tax records.
  • Enquiries that did not become lessons — up to 12 months.
  • Server logs — up to 12 months.

8. Your rights

Under data-protection law you may have the right to: access the data we hold about you; correct inaccurate data; delete your data; restrict or object to processing; and receive your data in a portable format. To exercise any of these, email hello@articulate.ee. We respond within 30 days.

You also have the right to complain to a supervisory authority.

9. Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will post the updated version on this page and change the "last updated" date above.

10. Contact

For any privacy question, contact us at hello@articulate.ee or use the details on our Impressum.
