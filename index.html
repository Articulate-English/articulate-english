Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get started?▼
Choose a plan above and book your first session. We'll confirm your teacher, send your learning materials, and run a quick placement check so every session is targeted from day one.
What exams do you prepare for?▼
We cover IELTS, TOEFL, OET, CAE, CFE, IGCSE, Cambridge exams, PTE, Duolingo English Test, and CELPIP. If it's a major English exam, we have materials and strategies for it.
How does AI translation work during lessons?▼
When a concept can't be explained in English alone, your teacher can instantly translate key terms into your home language using integrated AI tools. This saves you from having to look up words after class and keeps the lesson flowing.
Can I lock in my current rate?▼
Current rates are guaranteed for the duration of your plan. If you commit to 12 months upfront we can offer a reduced rate — request the details when you enquire.
Do you offer corporate training?▼
Yes. We offer custom corporate contracts starting at $2,500/month with dedicated account managers, bulk pricing, custom content, and team progress dashboards. Request a proposal above.
Do you teach groups or only 1-on-1?▼
Every session on this site is dedicated 1-on-1 with your teacher. Group formats are available for corporate clients under a separate team contract.
What does FL0-FL13 mean?▼
Our proprietary Fluency Level system ranges from FL0 (absolute beginner) to FL13 (near-native). Every level has clear milestones, assessments, and learning outcomes so you always know exactly where you stand.
