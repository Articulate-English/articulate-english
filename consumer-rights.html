Consumer rights & withdrawal

Your right to change your mind, in plain English. Applies to consumers in the EU and the UK.

Plain English summary

  • If you are a consumer in the EU or the UK, you generally have 14 days to withdraw from a lesson contract, for any reason.
  • For services, the 14 days run from the day you conclude the contract — not from the day the lesson takes place.
  • If your lesson has already been fully delivered, you lose the right to withdraw only if you gave a specific consent for us to start early (see below).
  • If you asked us to start early and then withdraw, you may owe a proportionate amount for what was delivered.
  • Swiss consumers: Swiss law gives no general right of withdrawal for online purchases. Our own cancellation terms apply instead.

Who this applies to

This page explains the rights that mandatory consumer law gives to people buying lessons as private consumers in the European Union and the United Kingdom. If you buy as a business, or you live outside the EU/UK, your contract is governed by our Terms of Service and the law stated there.

These are legal rights. You cannot sign them away, and a clause in our terms that tries to remove them would be unenforceable.

Your right to withdraw (EU / UK)

You have 14 days to withdraw from the contract without giving a reason.

The consent we ask for (Article 16(a))

To let you start a lesson before the 14-day period ends, we ask for a separate, specific consent. It is not part of our general terms and you do not have to give it — you can always wait until the 14 days have passed.

Consent wording shown at payment and booking:

"I want my lesson to take place before the 14-day withdrawal period ends, and I understand that once the lesson has been fully performed I lose my right of withdrawal."

If you give this consent and the lesson is delivered in full, the withdrawal right ends. If you do not give it, we will not start the lesson inside the 14-day period.

Cancellation policy vs. your statutory right

Our booking terms allow free cancellation or rescheduling more than 24 hours before a lesson. That is a contractual policy we choose to offer. It is separate from, and does not replace, your statutory right to withdraw. Where the law gives you a stronger right, the law wins.

Switzerland

Swiss law provides no general right of withdrawal for online purchases. Our own terms apply: free cancellation more than 24 hours before the lesson, and a refund of any unused lessons you have paid for. A Swiss doorstep-style sale (for example an unsolicited offer made to you by phone or video call, over CHF 100) carries its own 14-day right under Articles 40a ff. of the Swiss Code of Obligations.
