Delivery & refunds

How your lessons reach you, and how money is handled if something goes wrong.

How lessons are delivered

  • All lessons are live, one-to-one video sessions — there is no physical product and nothing is shipped.
  • Lessons run on the video-conferencing platform we name at booking. You join from a browser; on most machines no install is needed, and it works on Linux and older computers.
  • Booking shows the time in your own time zone, and the confirmation states it again. You are responsible for joining at the correct local time.
  • Your confirmation includes a calendar invitation you can add to your own calendar.
  • Materials for your level are made available to you through the account area and by email.
  • Where a lesson recording is part of your plan, it is made available to the booking account, not sent as a file attachment by default.

What you need

  • A working camera and microphone, and a stable internet connection.
  • A quiet place to talk.
  • Nothing to install in most cases — a current browser is enough.

If technology fails

If a session cannot go ahead because of a fault on our side (for example our connection or equipment fails), we reschedule it at no cost. If the fault is on yours, we will still try to make the time useful — for example by switching to audio or by moving the remaining minutes to another slot.

Where a third-party service (the video platform or the payment provider) fails for reasons outside our control, we reschedule rather than refund, as set out in our Terms of Service.

Cancellations and rescheduling

More than 24 hours beforeFree to cancel or reschedule. Any credit stays on your account.
Less than 24 hours beforeThe lesson counts as used, unless we can fill the slot.
No-showIf you do not join within 10 minutes of the start time, the lesson is treated as used.
We cancelThe lesson is rescheduled at no cost, or refunded at your request.

This is our contractual cancellation policy. It does not replace any statutory right you may have to withdraw — see Consumer rights & withdrawal.

Refunds

  • Unused lessons in a block: if you have paid for a block and not used all of it, ask and we refund the unused portion.
  • Timing: refunds go back to the original payment method within 14 days of your request being accepted.
  • Monthly plans: a monthly plan covers one calendar month from purchase. Unused lessons do not roll over unless we agree in writing. You can stop a recurring plan at any time; it ends at the close of the month you have paid for.
  • How a refund is paid: through the payment provider you paid with. We cannot pay a refund to a different card or account.
  • Statutory rights: where consumer law gives you a stronger refund right, that right applies.

Chargebacks and disputes

Please contact us before opening a dispute with your bank. Most problems are solved in a message, and a chargeback adds fees and delay for both of us. If a dispute is raised, we respond with the booking record, the delivery record and any consent you gave.

Credits

Where we owe you time rather than money, we add a credit to your account. Credits have no expiry unless we tell you otherwise in writing.
