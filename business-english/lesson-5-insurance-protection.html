Question:

Any challenges setting up accounts abroad?

Documentation requirements can be extensive. Language barriers complicate forms. Fees are often higher for foreigners. Local references help expedite processes.

Discussion:

How has digital banking changed services?

Apps now handle most transactions. Branch visits are rarer but more specialized. Security concerns increase with digital solutions. Accessibility improves for rural areas.
