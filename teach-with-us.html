Teach with Articulate English

A platform for English teachers who would rather spend their time teaching than preparing materials, marketing and doing admin.

The problem we are solving for teachers

Most experienced teachers lose hours every week to three things: building materials from scratch, finding students, and handling scheduling and payments. None of those are teaching.

Articulate English is putting the teaching first. We are building a platform and a curated materials library that teachers can work from, with the admin handled, so the job stays what you trained for.

What you would get

A real materials library

Original CEFR-graded curriculum material and exam-simulation speaking practice — B2 (FCE) and IELTS speaking, IELTS writing, and structured A1–C1 course material. Written in-house, not scraped from past papers.

Admin handled

Booking, scheduling and payments run through the platform, so you are not chasing invoices or rescheduling over email.

Your own students, or ours

Bring the students you already have, teach the ones we send you, or both. You decide how full your timetable is.

Your own style

The materials are a resource, not a script. You teach in the way that works for you and for the student in front of you.

How it works

  • 1. You tell us about your experience and how you like to teach.
  • 2. We meet, look at the materials together, and agree whether it is a fit.
  • 3. You teach from the library, in your own style, at hours you choose.
  • 4. You pay a per-lesson platform fee plus a materials fee. The more you teach and use in a month, the lower the rate becomes.

The exact rates are being finalised with the first teachers. We will always set them out in writing before anyone commits to anything.

Who it is for

  • Teachers with real classroom or online experience.
  • CELTA, TEFL or equivalent — or a degree plus substantial teaching hours.
  • A reliable internet connection, a quiet space and a webcam.
  • People who want steady work without being told how to teach it.

What we are clear about

  • This is not employment. Teachers on the platform work independently, not as employees of Articulate English. Your status, tax and insurance are your own responsibility, and we will set out the arrangement in writing.
  • This is not a school hiring staff. We are a platform and a materials library, not an employer.
  • We are onboarding a small first group. We would rather work properly with a handful of teachers than badly with fifty.

Register your interest

Tell us where you are and what you teach. We will reply with the current terms and, if it looks like a fit, a short call. No obligation.

We store your address and location only so we can reply and, only if you ticked the box, send occasional news. See our privacy policy. We never sell your data.

Questions teachers ask

Do I have to use your materials?

You can bring your own, use ours, or mix them. Most teachers start with our exam-simulation material because it is the part that takes the longest to build.

Do you find students for me?

Yes, as the school grows — and you can bring your own students too. We will be honest with you about how many we can realistically send.

Am I an employee?

No. Teachers work independently. We will put the arrangement in writing so there is no ambiguity about status, tax or insurance.

What does it cost me?

A per-lesson platform fee plus a materials fee, which falls as you teach and use more in a month. Exact figures are set out in writing before you commit.

When does it start?

We are onboarding a small first group. Register your interest and we will tell you where we are.

Can I teach children?

Yes, with the same safeguards we apply to our own teaching: under-18 students are booked by a parent or guardian. See our safeguarding policy.
